About KPP HOA

Katatagang Pagkakaisa ng Pamayanan Homeowners Association, Inc.

Our Organization

KPP HOA, Inc. is a homeowners association committed to representing and serving its community through responsible governance, member participation, and organized community programs.

🎯 Our Mission

To promote unity, protect the interests of homeowners, and provide responsive community services through transparent and accountable leadership.

🌱 Our Vision

A peaceful, progressive, organized, and empowered community where residents work together for the common good.
